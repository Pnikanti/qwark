- 2026-08-15 — Rest-end cue made loud enough to be a cue. The beep was two 0,25-gain sine tones over 0,25s — close to the quietest sound a browser can make, because a sine has no harmonics and a phone speaker reproduces almost nothing of a pure tone at that level. It is now three rising square tones, lowpassed at 5 kHz so the harmonics carry without turning to hiss, through a limiter at 0,85 master gain: measured in an `OfflineAudioContext`, +16,0 dB RMS and roughly twice as long. 0,9 was tried first and overshoots to a clipping 1,006 peak; 0,85 peaks at −0,3 dBFS.
